Communication Gap Definitions & Logic

A. Unanswered Question

Rule: Detects interrogative messages (ending with ? or interrogative words) where subsequent responses from other participants do not provide an answer, time, location, or topical alignment.

Neha: What time is the meeting?
Ravi: I am working on the report.
Soham: I'll send the dataset.
→ Flagged: Question received no direct response.
B. Repeated Request

Rule: Detects action/resource requests (e.g., Can you send, Please provide) that are repeated across messages without fulfillment or resolution in between.

Ravi: Can you send the dataset?
Soham: Okay.
Ravi: Can you send the dataset?
→ Flagged: Request repeated without resolution.
C. Repeated Clarification

Rule: Detects repetitive expressions of ambiguity or confusion (e.g., What do you mean?, Which one?, I don't understand), signaling an ongoing communication roadblock.

Ravi: What do you mean?
Alex: Just change the core modules.
Soham: Which one?
→ Flagged: Multiple clarification requests detected.
D. Unresolved Topic

Rule: Identifies key topics or proposals introduced (e.g., about X, regarding X, update on X) that receive no concluding agreement, decision, or follow-up before the conversation ends.

Neha: What about the budget for Q4?
Ravi: Let's focus on the demo today.
→ Flagged: Topic introduced but no resolution found.
